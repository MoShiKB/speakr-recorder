# Speakr Recorder: privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-22_

Speakr Recorder is a Chrome extension that records audio you choose to record
and uploads it to a Speakr server that you run and configure yourself.

**What it records.** Only when you press Record: the audio of the chosen tab,
or everything the computer plays, together with your microphone. It never
records on its own. Screen sharing is used only to reach the computer's sound;
the picture is not saved.

**Where the data goes.** Recordings are stored on your computer (in the
extension's local storage) until they are uploaded to the Speakr server
address you enter in the settings. They are sent nowhere else: no analytics,
no third parties, and the developer has no copy.

**What is stored.** Your settings, including the Speakr server address and
API token, are kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer.
Recordings can be deleted from the extension's list at any time; by default
the local copy is deleted once your server has it.

**The optional Windows helper.** If you install it, it runs on your computer
and passes the computer's sound to the extension while you record. It sends
nothing over the network.

**Contact.** Use the developer email shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
